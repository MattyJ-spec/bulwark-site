# Changelog and credits

Every change to the rules, the engine or the pages is listed here as a dated entry, newest first. Presets never change in place: a rule change is a new dated version, so the record shows what the rule was on any given day. Findings from testers are credited by first name or handle, with permission.

## 2026-11-22 — v0.3.9 (the refusals and STOP-line changes promised below; amber "not for" lines; Topstep minimum days; presets v1.11; engine v0.2.2 unchanged)
**No loss limit, profit target or reset time changes in this release.** It changes what the pages refuse and what they call "OK", and it adds cautions for accounts a page is not for. Most of the page code was written and audited on 15–16 November and held back when the correction of 21 November went ahead of it; the last bullet says how it was reviewed.
- **A The5ers High Stakes page no longer calculates for an account size it does not cover.** Promised in the entry below. The5ers' Program Rules table gives limits to six account sizes. The *$2.5K to $25K* page now shows figures for $2,500, $5,000, $10,000 and $25,000 only: until this release a $50,000 or $100,000 typed there was calculated at 5% and 10%, more room than The5ers gives those sizes. The *$50K and $100K* page shows figures for $50,000 and $100,000 only. Each page says which sizes it covers under the size box; for any other size it shows no figures, says why and, where there is one, points to the other calculator. **Neither page has been checked for a scaled-up account.** The5ers' Scaling Plan raises a High Stakes account step by step, up to $500,000, and the High Stakes pages we read do not state a scaled-up account's loss limits: each page now says so in its amber line, and a size the table does not list ($7,500 or $60,000, say) gets no figures. The refusal goes by the account-size box. A balance typed into the day-start boxes while that box still holds another size is still calculated: the daily floor and the STOP line from that balance at this page's percentage, the max-loss floor and the target from the size in the box.
- **Entries that cannot be read stop the page.** Promised in the v0.3.7 entry. An entry a number box cannot read — letters ("25k"), brackets ("(1500)"), separators that fit no number format ("1,2345", "104'000", "- 1200") — or that has two readings — one dot followed by exactly three digits in a money box ("104.000": 104 or 104,000?) or in the stop-distance and value boxes ("1.500": 1.5 or 1,500?), one comma followed by exactly three digits where it could be a decimal ("0,125" in any box; "1,250" in a box that is not a money amount) — used to be replaced by the box's default or misread, silently. The page now names the entry and gives no size until it is fixed; when the entry is one of the account figures or the trade list it shows no figures at all, and when it is one of the three sizing boxes the floors stay on screen and only the size is withheld. Nothing the boxes still read is read differently from v0.3.7 (a leading "$", the printed minus sign, space or comma thousands separators, a decimal comma). A percentage box still reads "1.500" as one and a half, the only thing it can mean there; a figure of one to three digits, a dot and three digits in the stop-distance or value box ("6.452") is refused like "1.500", and the message offers the two forms the box accepts (6452 or 6.4520); "0.125" is read. Every example the v0.3.7 entry below lists as still open is now refused by name. Two more refusals were added after this release's review (last item below): in the trade list, a figure of one to three digits, a dot and exactly three digits ("-1.500": a loss of 1.50 or of 1,500?) is refused as ambiguous, as it is in the money boxes — until now the list read it as 1.50; and a figure too long to be a number is refused by name.
- **"NO BUFFER" instead of "OK" when nothing is held back above a floor.** The STOP line holds a buffer back above the daily limit, measured on equity, and the size is taken from the room above that line. Three states had no buffer and still read "OK — trade your plan" (or, on a page with no daily limit, its usual "No daily loss limit…" line): the max-loss floor nearer than the daily STOP line (after a pullback on a trailing account, or deep in drawdown on any account); the buffer slider at 100%; and, at firms that test a floor on balance *or* equity (FundingPips, FundedNext), a closed balance near the floor while floating profit holds equity up — there the size could use room the balance did not have, so a full stop could breach on balance. Two of these — the max-loss floor nearer than the daily STOP line, and a closed balance near a floor that is tested on balance or equity — are the first two limits the v0.3.7 entry below names as unchanged in that release. The page now shows NO BUFFER with the distance to the nearest floor on the measure the firm tests, and never sizes to more than that distance: it gives a size only when the risk you ask for is smaller — and says how much a full stop would leave above the floor — and no size at all otherwise, because a full stop would touch it. Holding a real buffer above the nearer floor, and sizing on the tested measure, are engine changes; they are in the engine backlog and have to pass both engines before they ship.
- **A balance exactly on a floor reads breached.** Cents that add up to a floor could land a hair above it inside the arithmetic, so a page could show equity and floor as the same figure and still say "OK". The banner now goes by the cent-rounded figures the page displays: on the floor is breached. This is the third limit the v0.3.7 entry below names.
- **Futures pages size in whole contracts (Topstep, FTMO Futures).** These pages used to open with a forex example in the sizing boxes and print a fractional "lots" figure. They now start with the stop distance and the contract's dollar value per point empty — the page refuses to size until you enter both — and give a whole number of contracts, rounded down. Topstep's and FTMO's contract limits are not applied, and the pages say so beside the figure. Each fixed-size page now links to the other sizes beside its size box.
- **A daily limit that pauses the day reads as a pause (Topstep).** Where an account has Topstep's Daily Loss Limit, reaching it stops trading until the next session and does not fail the account. The banner used to read "DAILY LIMIT BREACHED" in the failure colour; it now says the limit was reached, that there is no trading until the next session, and that this limit does not fail the account.
- **Topstep ×3 — minimum trading days 2; the Daily Loss Limit is optional; not for a Static Trading Combine.** Topstep's Trading Combine Parameters table lists a minimum of 2 trading days. These calculators carried none (a sentence said so from 21 November); new dated versions carry 2 and cite that table. Each page now says in an amber line that the Daily Loss Limit is optional at Topstep — the page applies the amount Topstep offers at checkout ($1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000); **if your account has no Daily Loss Limit, the daily figures and the STOP line on the page do not apply to it** — and that the page is not for a Static Trading Combine, whose Maximum Loss Limit never moves. The pages cannot yet be told that an account has no Daily Loss Limit: on such an account their STOP line and size are tighter than its rules require. Loss limits and targets are unchanged.
- **Amber "not for" lines — which accounts a page is not for.** *FundingPips:* the two 2-Step Standard pages (5% and 3% daily) and the two 1-Step Flex pages (2% and 3%) each say which daily-limit configuration they are for and link to the other; the 1-Step Flex pages also say they are not for a 1 Step Labs account (1% daily; a 3% maximum loss that trails at end of day) or a Zero account (3% daily; a 5% trailing maximum loss) — either would be shown more room than it has. *FTMO 1-Step ×2:* not for an FTMO Instant account. FTMO Instant has the same 3% daily limit, but its maximum loss limit follows the highest closed balance in real time — it rises the moment a trade closes at a new high — where the 1-Step's is set once a day; after a winning trade a 1-Step page would show an Instant account more room than it has. Its consistency limit is 30%, not 50%. *FundedNext Stellar Lite:* not for an account bought with the Maximum Loss Limit 10% add-on, which raises the limit from 8% to 10%; the page uses 8%. *The5ers High Stakes ×2:* not checked for a scaled-up account (first item above). Not every look-alike has a line yet: the FTMO 2-Step page has none for FTMO Instant (3% daily, where that page uses 5%), the three FundedNext Stellar pages have none for Stellar Instant (no daily limit; a 6% trailing maximum loss), and the FTMO Futures Growth pages have none for FTMO Futures' Pro plan (which has a Daily Loss Limit). These lines are still to be added.
- **FundedNext Stellar ×3 — add-ons and Quick Strike stated; newer sources.** Each page now names the No Minimum Trading Days add-on (sold on the $6K, $15K and $25K Stellar 1-Step and 2-Step accounts, and on Stellar Lite) and says that FundedNext's Quick Strike requirement — the share of profit from trades closed within 30 seconds "must be maintained below 30% at all times" — is not modelled. FundedNext's add-ons, its payout options and six of its help-centre articles, dated 30 September to 5 November, had not been read by us before 21 November; they were found that day, after v0.3.8 was published. No floor on this site was wrong because of them. The articles for each model, the add-on article and the model's own product page are now among that page's sources.
- **What was read for this release.** On 22 November, in a browser, with every account size, plan and option selected: The5ers' High Stakes and Summer Plan pages and the two High Stakes FAQ articles — and, later the same day, the High Stakes page's Scaling Plan table and three more High Stakes articles (growth, scaling and fixed payout, general rules), after this release's reviewer pointed to a table our first read had not recorded; FundingPips' four current model articles, its Compare page, its pricing configurator and its Legacy Rules page, every tab; FTMO's trading-objectives page (each product and each objective opened), its 1-Step page (every size) and its trading update of 8 October; Topstep's four articles; FundedNext's general rules, eleven help-centre articles and three product pages (every size). No limit on those pages differs from what the calculators use; what they do not state — a scaled-up High Stakes account's limits — is said in the first item above. The fourteen calculators that have new dated versions, and FundingPips 2-Step Flex and Pro, show 22 November. FTMO 2-Step, FTMO Futures Growth ×5 and the two Summer Plan calculators keep 21 November: not every page they cite was read again on the 22nd.
- Wording: each calculator's summary and the Compare table say what a trailing floor trails — the highest **end-of-day** balance (FTMO 1-Step, FTMO Futures, Topstep); the countdown says that it is the daily limit that resets.
- Seen on the firms' pages, with no calculator here: FundingPips 1 Step Labs and Zero, FTMO Instant, Topstep's Static Trading Combine, The5ers' Summer Plan 1-Step, a The5ers High Stakes account that has been scaled up, FundedNext Stellar Instant. Apex's calculators are still to come, in a separate release.
- Engine unchanged (v0.2.2), and its tests with it: 48 run and pass; two more are skipped where the sibling desk's fixture files are absent.
- **How this release was reviewed.** The page code — the refusals for unreadable entries, NO BUFFER, the exact touch, whole-contract sizing, the pause wording — was written on 15–16 November in a build that also held the Apex calculators, and an independent reviewer audited that build before clearing it. The first build was held back: among its findings, "OK" was shown with nothing held back above a floor, and the futures pages carried the forex example. The corrected build was reviewed again: on the balance-or-equity pages its new NO BUFFER size line still measured the room on equity (corrected, as described above), and a size could still be given at a 100% buffer when a full stop would land on a floor (now refused). That build was not published. This release is that page code on top of v0.3.8, with one addition — the account-size refusal. An independent reviewer audited the whole release before it was published and did not clear the first build: The5ers' High Stakes accounts can be scaled up to $500,000, which the refusal's wording had called another product; at a 100% buffer one rounding case still read "OK"; a figure too long to be a number was carried as infinity; and several sentences claimed more than the pages did. These were corrected — the High Stakes pages now give figures only for the six sizes The5ers' table lists, and the trade list refuses "-1.500" as the money boxes do — and the corrected build was looked at again, before publication. The second look asked for one more sentence to be reworded — the refusal had said that any High Stakes account at another size had been scaled up — and that was done and checked.

## 2026-11-16 — v0.3.7 (full re-read of every firm page the calculators cite; presets v1.9; engine v0.2.2 unchanged)
Corrections found by re-reading the firms' own pages on 15 November 2026, and by an independent reviewer who audited this release before it was published. **One of them is a floor**, and this site was behind on it from 5 November until this release.
- **FTMO Futures Growth $100K and $150K — the maximum drawdown was reduced, and these pages were showing the old figure.** FTMO's Futures Trading Update of 5 November 2026: for Evaluations created on or after Thursday 5 November 2026, 4:30 a.m. Eastern Time, the maximum drawdown is **$3,000** on the $100K (was $3,500) and **$4,500** on the $150K (was $5,000). Evaluations created before that moment keep the old figures, "including the Sim-Funded and Live Accounts they lead to". Until this release the two calculators showed $3,500 and $5,000 to everyone — **up to $500 more room than a trader on a new Evaluation actually had.** If you sized a trade from those pages on an Evaluation created since 5 November, re-check your floor against Account MetriX. Each size now has two calculators — *evaluations created from 5 Nov 2026* and *evaluations created before 5 Nov 2026* — each linking to the other. FTMO does not say which figure applies to the new account it issues after a Reset, or on the next billing date after a breach; the pages treat such an account as a new one — the tighter figure — until Account MetriX shows otherwise. The $50K is unchanged at $2,000.
- **FTMO Futures Growth — Consistency Rule now modelled; contract limit and scope stated.** FTMO's page lists the rule per plan: Growth **40%** (Pro 50%) — your best day may not be more than 40% of your total profit across all trading days in the Evaluation; exceeding it is not a breach, you keep trading until it fits. Our reads of 8 and 23 October recorded both percentages without the plan each belongs to, so until this release these pages told you to read it from your dashboard. The Growth pages now also state FTMO's contract limit (5 / 10 / 15 by size, which the sizing figure does not apply) and that they model the Evaluation only.
- **FundedNext Stellar Lite — minimum trading days 5 → 2.** For Challenges purchased or reset on or after 12 October 2026 the minimum is 2 trading days per phase; Challenges purchased or last reset before that date keep 5 (FundedNext help-centre article 8019803; the General Rules page lists 2). The floors are unchanged. The 23 October run recorded 5 and did not catch this.
- **FundedNext Stellar 2-Step, 1-Step and Lite — max loss now tested on balance *or* equity.** FundedNext's Stellar Lite article reads "Your account balance or equity cannot fall below 92% of your initial balance". These calculators tested equity only and called that the conservative reading; it is not when your closed balance is below your equity — a closed balance under the level, with floating profit holding equity above it, read "OK". They now flag a breach when either touches the level. The floors themselves have not moved.
- **FundingPips ×6 — reset time is seasonal.** FundingPips' articles now state the daily reset as 00:00 Platform Time with a *seasonal* offset: UTC+3 in summer, UTC+2 in winter (winter time from the first Sunday of November). The calculators had a fixed UTC+3, so since 1 November the countdown ran **an hour early**. It now follows 5:00 p.m. New York time — the same moment all year except the week between the first and second Sundays of March, when FundingPips' stated reset comes an hour before the countdown reaches zero; the pages say so. The floors are unchanged.
- **FundingPips — accounts bought before 28 September 2026. Found on 16 November, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** FundingPips keeps a Legacy Rules page for accounts bought before 28 September 2026 and not reset since. It was not among the pages re-read on 15 November: the weekly check's report of 9 November named it as unread, and it was first read on 16 November. For the models on this site, its loss limits are the ones on the 2-Step Standard (5% daily), 2-Step Flex, 2-Step Pro and 1-Step Flex (3% daily) pages, so no floor here is affected. Two objectives differ. 2-Step Flex: the legacy page has an 85% split where the current one has 80%, and profit targets of 10% / 6%; this site shows the 80% split and targets of 10% / 8%, a Phase 2 target higher than a legacy account's. 2-Step Pro: a legacy account needs 2 minimum trading days per phase (1 if bought before 26 August 2026 and never reset); this site says none for accounts bought from 26 August, as FundingPips' current article reads — its two pages differ for accounts bought from 26 August to 27 September. The legacy page also words the daily reset as 00:00 Platform Time (UTC+3), with no seasonal note. On an older account, read your targets, minimum days and reset time from your dashboard. The two pages are to be corrected in the next release.
- **FundingPips Master accounts — rules on the loss from a single trade idea are not modelled. Found on 16 November, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** FundingPips' current articles put Master accounts on the 100% split under its Striking System — a warning when the loss on one trade idea reaches 1% of the account, the fourth closing the account. Its Legacy Rules page applies the Striking System to more Master accounts and adds a Risk Per Trade Idea limit on some. These calculators do not model those rules, and the size they show does not apply them.
- **Input guards on every calculator.** Three ways a box left untouched could show a floor lower than the rule: on "higher of balance and equity" firms the instruction for the anchor sat under the wrong box; a day-start balance above the "highest end-of-day balance" box was not carried into the trailing floor; and a number typed with a space ("104 000") was read as 104. Now the anchor is never taken as less than the day-start balance, the highest end-of-day balance is never taken as less than the day-start balance, the account size is fixed on pages whose limits are dollar amounts, and a breached account shows a size of zero. The number boxes also read more of what people type: a space as a thousands separator where exactly three digits follow it ("104 000"); a single comma followed by one or two final digits as a decimal point ("0,5" and "98512,37" used to be read as 5 and 9,851,237); the minus sign this site itself prints ("−1200" used to be read as nothing at all); and a leading "$". A browser can put back what you typed on an earlier visit. Chromium-based browsers do it by position, so after a release that adds a box — this one adds one on the three FTMO Futures pages — a returning visitor's stop distance could reappear in the risk box. The calculators now tell the browser to keep nothing typed: a tab the browser has to load again starts from the page's own defaults. **Still open, and silent:** an entry a box cannot read at all — letters, brackets, usually a minus sign separated from the number by a space ("- 1200") — is replaced by that box's default; an entry it can read only in part — an apostrophe, or a space that is not followed by exactly three digits — is cut short ("104'000" is read as 104); a full stop used as a thousands separator ("104.000") is read as 104; and any comma other than a single comma before one or two final digits is a thousands separator ("0,125" is read as 125; "0,5%" as 5). A visible refusal for all of these is in the next release; until then, check the figures the page shows — "Live equity", and the risk in "risk allowed" — against what you typed.
- **Three limits of the STOP line, the size and the floor test — found on 15 November while the next release was being reviewed, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** *No buffer above a max-loss floor.* On programs with a daily limit the STOP line is the daily floor plus a safety buffer, and it is never placed below the max-loss floor. When the max-loss floor is higher than that buffered line, the STOP line is the floor itself: there is no buffer above it, the status reads "OK" until the floor is touched, and the size can be the whole of the room to the floor — a full stop on it lands on the floor, which these pages count as a breach. FTMO 2-Step $100K at a day-start balance of 90,400: STOP line 90,000 and "risk allowed 400.00", all of the room. *STOP status and size worked out from equity.* Where the max-loss floor is tested on the lower of closed balance and equity — FundingPips, and FundedNext Stellar from this release — the STOP status, the room shown beside it and the size are still worked out from your equity alone. With a floating profit open, your closed balance is nearer the floor than your equity is, and the status can read "OK" with the balance a cent above the floor. FundedNext Stellar Lite $100K, closed balance 92,400 with a floating profit of 1,000 — the page shows "risk allowed 467.00" where the balance is 400 above the 92,000 level, and a full stop of that size, once closed, leaves the balance at 91,933. *An exact touch.* An account that lands exactly on a floor, to the cent, is not always read as a breach when a closed trade and a floating figure are both typed, because of the way a computer adds cents: in a sweep of these pages, about one such touch in eight on the max-loss floor. With closed trades only, or a floating figure only, every exact touch in the same sweep was read as a breach, and one cent above a floor never was. FTMO 2-Step $100K: day-start balance 90,225.82, one closed trade of −247.98 and a floating 22.16 — "Live equity" 90,000, max-loss floor 90,000, status "OK". The two "risk allowed" figures above are at the pages' default risk of 0.5%. All three are changed in the next release; until then the size line takes neither of the first two into account.
- **The weekly check.** A scheduled weekly check was running. Its run of 9 November reported the lower drawdown figures on FTMO's page and asked for them to be confirmed in a browser; no correction followed until this release. Its runs were not logged here as this site had said they would be.
