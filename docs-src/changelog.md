# Changelog and credits

Every change to the rules, the engine or the pages is listed here as a dated entry, newest first. Presets never change in place: a rule change is a new dated version, so the record shows what the rule was on any given day. Findings from testers are credited by first name or handle, with permission.

## 2026-11-21 — v0.3.8 (correction: The5ers High Stakes by account size; FundingPips older accounts; presets v1.10; engine v0.2.2 unchanged)
A correction release. **One of the corrections is a floor: on the two larger The5ers High Stakes account sizes this site showed more room than the firm gives.** It was found on 21 November 2026, while every page the calculators cite was being read again. The release was audited by an independent reviewer before it was published; the first build did not pass — it pointed Summer Plan holders to a High Stakes page — and the Summer Plan cautions described below were added before publication.
- **The5ers High Stakes — $50K and $100K accounts have a 4% daily and an 8% maximum loss, and this site showed 5% and 10%.** The Program Rules table on The5ers' High Stakes page changes with the account size selected: $2.5K, $5K, $10K and $25K accounts have a maximum daily loss of 5% and a maximum loss of 10%; **$50K and $100K accounts have 4% and 8%**. This site's calculator showed 5% and 10% for every size, and it opened on a $100,000 account. On a $100K account that put the max-loss floor at $90,000 where The5ers' table gives $92,000 — **$2,000 more room than the account has** — and made the daily allowance $1,000 too large; on a $50K account the differences are $1,000 and $500. If you sized a trade or judged your room from that page on a $50K or $100K High Stakes account, check your floors against your dashboard. There are now two calculators — *$50K and $100K accounts*, at the old address so that anyone returning to it sees the tighter limits, and *$2.5K to $25K accounts* — each linking to the other. The account-size box on the $2.5K to $25K page still accepts any figure; a larger size typed there is calculated at 5% and 10%. The page says so above the inputs. A refusal in the page itself follows in the next release.
- **How it was missed, and for how long.** Our reads of that page on 8 and 23 October and on 15 November recorded the table for the account size shown first and did not select each size. We do not know the day the table first showed 4% and 8%. The page carries no date; the record of that page in The5ers' own site data, which holds this table, was last modified on 8 October 2026. So the table has very probably shown these figures since at least that day, and this site has very probably been wrong on those two sizes for at least as long — through our reads of 23 October and 15 November — and possibly longer. On 16 November 2026 The5ers rewrote its two FAQ articles on these limits to say that they vary by account ("please verify the specific parameters on the Program Rules page for the account you hold or plan to purchase"); until then both gave 10% and 5% without a range. The entry below lists The5ers High Stakes as "unchanged, re-read on 15 November": that read was of one account size. The weekly automated check reads the page as it first loads; its run of 16 November quoted 5% and 10% from it. The5ers' pages do not say whether an account bought earlier keeps other limits — read yours from your dashboard.
- **Reading rule changed.** A rules table with a selector for account size, plan or option is now read with every choice selected, and recorded for each. Every other firm's pages were checked for the same thing on 21 November: FTMO states its 2-Step and 1-Step objectives once, as percentages, for every size; FundedNext's general rules say "Same rules for every trader"; FundingPips states its limits per model; FTMO Futures and Topstep already have one calculator per account size. FundedNext's and FundingPips' own product pages, read with every account size selected, show the same percentages at every size. No other limit that depends on the account size was found. The Summer Plan's own program page, with a selector for six 2-Step options, had never been cited or read by us; it was found and read, every option, during this release's review, and it confirms 3% and 10% for every 2-Step option.
- **The5ers 2-Step Plan ×2 — renamed, rules unchanged; and both High Stakes pages now say they are not for Summer Plan accounts.** These two calculators (3% daily, 10% maximum) are the 2-Step plan of The5ers' Summer Plan and are now named so; they were labelled "standard plan" and carried a banner saying High Stakes is 5%. The Summer Plan's program page gives the same two limits for the $50K, $100K and $200K options. The5ers' FAQ for the $50K Summer Plan words the 2-Step options' maximum loss differently from its table; the Classic page says so. The5ers' FAQ has also called the $200K option the "High Stakes Summer Plan", and its High Stakes FAQ now says the maximum daily loss "Ranges between 3% and 5%", where the High Stakes table shows 4% or 5% — so a Summer Plan trader may look for the account under High Stakes. **A Summer Plan account has a 3% daily limit, tighter than either High Stakes page shows.** Both High Stakes pages now say they are not for Summer Plan accounts and link to these two calculators; these two say that a High Stakes account has other limits, link back, and say that the Summer Plan's 1-Step plan (6% maximum loss) is not covered.
- **FundingPips 2-Step Flex and 2-Step Pro — accounts bought before 28 September 2026.** Promised in the entry below. FundingPips' Legacy Rules page was read in full, every tab, on 21 November. Its loss limits are the same as the current ones for every model on this site, so no floor is affected. Two objectives differ, and both pages now say so: on 2-Step Flex a legacy account has an 85% split and profit targets of 10% / 6%, against the 10% / 8% the page shows; on 2-Step Pro a legacy account needs 2 minimum trading days per phase (1 if bought before 26 August 2026), and FundingPips' own two pages differ for accounts bought from 26 August to 27 September. Both pages also say that the legacy page still words the daily reset as 00:00 Platform Time (UTC+3). The figures they calculate with are unchanged.
- **FundingPips ×6 — single-trade-idea rules stated as not modelled.** Each FundingPips page now says that FundingPips' rules on the loss from a single trade idea on Master accounts — its Striking System on the on-demand 100% split and, under its Legacy Rules, further limits on some accounts — are not modelled, and that the size shown does not apply them.
- **Topstep ×3 — a note; figures unchanged.** Topstep changed its Maximum Loss Limit and Consistency articles on 19 November 2026. The figures and rules these calculators use are the same. The Maximum Loss Limit article now also describes a Static Trading Combine, whose limit never moves; the three calculators model the trailing limit of the original Trading Combine, and each page now says so. Topstep's Trading Combine Parameters table, which these calculators had not cited, was read during this release's review: its figures are the same, and it lists a minimum of 2 trading days, which each page now mentions.
- **Two things the entry below promised for "the next release" are not in this one.** The visible refusal for entries a number box cannot read, and the three changes to the STOP line, the size and the floor test, were built and reviewed together with the Apex calculators. This correction was put ahead of that release because a loss limit on a live page was wrong. They follow in the release after this one; until then the cautions in the entry below stand.

## 2026-11-16 — v0.3.7 (full re-read of every firm page the calculators cite; presets v1.9; engine v0.2.2 unchanged)
Corrections found by re-reading the firms' own pages on 15 November 2026, and by an independent reviewer who audited this release before it was published. **One of them is a floor**, and this site was behind on it from 5 November until this release.
- **FTMO Futures Growth $100K and $150K — the maximum drawdown was reduced, and these pages were showing the old figure.** FTMO's Futures Trading Update of 5 November 2026: for Evaluations created on or after Thursday 5 November 2026, 4:30 a.m. Eastern Time, the maximum drawdown is **$3,000** on the $100K (was $3,500) and **$4,500** on the $150K (was $5,000). Evaluations created before that moment keep the old figures, "including the Sim-Funded and Live Accounts they lead to". Until this release the two calculators showed $3,500 and $5,000 to everyone — **up to $500 more room than a trader on a new Evaluation actually had.** If you sized a trade from those pages on an Evaluation created since 5 November, re-check your floor against Account MetriX. Each size now has two calculators — *evaluations created from 5 Nov 2026* and *evaluations created before 5 Nov 2026* — each linking to the other. FTMO does not say which figure applies to the new account it issues after a Reset, or on the next billing date after a breach; the pages treat such an account as a new one — the tighter figure — until Account MetriX shows otherwise. The $50K is unchanged at $2,000.
- **FTMO Futures Growth — Consistency Rule now modelled; contract limit and scope stated.** FTMO's page lists the rule per plan: Growth **40%** (Pro 50%) — your best day may not be more than 40% of your total profit across all trading days in the Evaluation; exceeding it is not a breach, you keep trading until it fits. Our reads of 8 and 23 October recorded both percentages without the plan each belongs to, so until this release these pages told you to read it from your dashboard. The Growth pages now also state FTMO's contract limit (5 / 10 / 15 by size, which the sizing figure does not apply) and that they model the Evaluation only.
- **FundedNext Stellar Lite — minimum trading days 5 → 2.** For Challenges purchased or reset on or after 12 October 2026 the minimum is 2 trading days per phase; Challenges purchased or last reset before that date keep 5 (FundedNext help-centre article 8019803; the General Rules page lists 2). The floors are unchanged. The 23 October run recorded 5 and did not catch this.
- **FundedNext Stellar 2-Step, 1-Step and Lite — max loss now tested on balance *or* equity.** FundedNext's Stellar Lite article reads "Your account balance or equity cannot fall below 92% of your initial balance". These calculators tested equity only and called that the conservative reading; it is not when your closed balance is below your equity — a closed balance under the level, with floating profit holding equity above it, read "OK". They now flag a breach when either touches the level. The floors themselves have not moved.
- **FundingPips ×6 — reset time is seasonal.** FundingPips' articles now state the daily reset as 00:00 Platform Time with a *seasonal* offset: UTC+3 in summer, UTC+2 in winter (winter time from the first Sunday of November). The calculators had a fixed UTC+3, so since 1 November the countdown ran **an hour early**. It now follows 5:00 p.m. New York time — the same moment all year except the week between the first and second Sundays of March, when FundingPips' stated reset comes an hour before the countdown reaches zero; the pages say so. The floors are unchanged.
- **FundingPips — accounts bought before 28 September 2026. Found on 16 November, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** FundingPips keeps a Legacy Rules page for accounts bought before 28 September 2026 and not reset since. It was not among the pages re-read on 15 November: the weekly check's report of 9 November named it as unread, and it was first read on 16 November. For the models on this site, its loss limits are the ones on the 2-Step Standard (5% daily), 2-Step Flex, 2-Step Pro and 1-Step Flex (3% daily) pages, so no floor here is affected. Two objectives differ. 2-Step Flex: the legacy page has an 85% split where the current one has 80%, and profit targets of 10% / 6%; this site shows the 80% split and targets of 10% / 8%, a Phase 2 target higher than a legacy account's. 2-Step Pro: a legacy account needs 2 minimum trading days per phase (1 if bought before 26 August 2026 and never reset); this site says none for accounts bought from 26 August, as FundingPips' current article reads — its two pages differ for accounts bought from 26 August to 27 September. The legacy page also words the daily reset as 00:00 Platform Time (UTC+3), with no seasonal note. On an older account, read your targets, minimum days and reset time from your dashboard. The two pages are to be corrected in the next release.
- **FundingPips Master accounts — rules on the loss from a single trade idea are not modelled. Found on 16 November, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** FundingPips' current articles put Master accounts on the 100% split under its Striking System — a warning when the loss on one trade idea reaches 1% of the account, the fourth closing the account. Its Legacy Rules page applies the Striking System to more Master accounts and adds a Risk Per Trade Idea limit on some. These calculators do not model those rules, and the size they show does not apply them.
- **Input guards on every calculator.** Three ways a box left untouched could show a floor lower than the rule: on "higher of balance and equity" firms the instruction for the anchor sat under the wrong box; a day-start balance above the "highest end-of-day balance" box was not carried into the trailing floor; and a number typed with a space ("104 000") was read as 104. Now the anchor is never taken as less than the day-start balance, the highest end-of-day balance is never taken as less than the day-start balance, the account size is fixed on pages whose limits are dollar amounts, and a breached account shows a size of zero. The number boxes also read more of what people type: a space as a thousands separator where exactly three digits follow it ("104 000"); a single comma followed by one or two final digits as a decimal point ("0,5" and "98512,37" used to be read as 5 and 9,851,237); the minus sign this site itself prints ("−1200" used to be read as nothing at all); and a leading "$". A browser can put back what you typed on an earlier visit. Chromium-based browsers do it by position, so after a release that adds a box — this one adds one on the three FTMO Futures pages — a returning visitor's stop distance could reappear in the risk box. The calculators now tell the browser to keep nothing typed: a tab the browser has to load again starts from the page's own defaults. **Still open, and silent:** an entry a box cannot read at all — letters, brackets, usually a minus sign separated from the number by a space ("- 1200") — is replaced by that box's default; an entry it can read only in part — an apostrophe, or a space that is not followed by exactly three digits — is cut short ("104'000" is read as 104); a full stop used as a thousands separator ("104.000") is read as 104; and any comma other than a single comma before one or two final digits is a thousands separator ("0,125" is read as 125; "0,5%" as 5). A visible refusal for all of these is in the next release; until then, check the figures the page shows — "Live equity", and the risk in "risk allowed" — against what you typed.
- **Three limits of the STOP line, the size and the floor test — found on 15 November while the next release was being reviewed, the same on this site before this release, and not changed by it.** *No buffer above a max-loss floor.* On programs with a daily limit the STOP line is the daily floor plus a safety buffer, and it is never placed below the max-loss floor. When the max-loss floor is higher than that buffered line, the STOP line is the floor itself: there is no buffer above it, the status reads "OK" until the floor is touched, and the size can be the whole of the room to the floor — a full stop on it lands on the floor, which these pages count as a breach. FTMO 2-Step $100K at a day-start balance of 90,400: STOP line 90,000 and "risk allowed 400.00", all of the room. *STOP status and size worked out from equity.* Where the max-loss floor is tested on the lower of closed balance and equity — FundingPips, and FundedNext Stellar from this release — the STOP status, the room shown beside it and the size are still worked out from your equity alone. With a floating profit open, your closed balance is nearer the floor than your equity is, and the status can read "OK" with the balance a cent above the floor. FundedNext Stellar Lite $100K, closed balance 92,400 with a floating profit of 1,000 — the page shows "risk allowed 467.00" where the balance is 400 above the 92,000 level, and a full stop of that size, once closed, leaves the balance at 91,933. *An exact touch.* An account that lands exactly on a floor, to the cent, is not always read as a breach when a closed trade and a floating figure are both typed, because of the way a computer adds cents: in a sweep of these pages, about one such touch in eight on the max-loss floor. With closed trades only, or a floating figure only, every exact touch in the same sweep was read as a breach, and one cent above a floor never was. FTMO 2-Step $100K: day-start balance 90,225.82, one closed trade of −247.98 and a floating 22.16 — "Live equity" 90,000, max-loss floor 90,000, status "OK". The two "risk allowed" figures above are at the pages' default risk of 0.5%. All three are changed in the next release; until then the size line takes neither of the first two into account.
- **The weekly check.** A scheduled weekly check was running. Its run of 9 November reported the lower drawdown figures on FTMO's page and asked for them to be confirmed in a browser; no correction followed until this release. Its runs were not logged here as this site had said they would be. The statements on this site about weekly checks have been replaced by what is actually done: rule pages are re-read page by page at a desk before a release that changes rule data — in a browser where the automated reader cannot read the page — and each calculator shows the date its firm's page was last read in full; that date changes only on a real read.
- **The5ers 2-Step ×2** — rules unchanged; the amber banner cited the firm's rules page at its September date, and the page is now dated 2 November 2026 with the same text. Re-issued so the banner cites the current date.
- Unchanged, re-read on 15 November: FTMO 2-Step and 1-Step ×2 (page modified 2 November; FTMO's six trading updates since 8 October change no Challenge rule); FundedNext Instant (still withheld); The5ers High Stakes; Topstep ×3. Apex's five help-centre pages were re-read and are unchanged; its calculators follow in a separate release — the earlier note that they needed an engine addition was wrong (each daily limit works out to the cent as a percentage of the account size).
- Engine unchanged (v0.2.2); six tests added (FTMO's own worked examples for the new $100K and $150K figures, the difference between the two groups, the 40% consistency examples, the FundedNext balance-or-equity case, and the FundingPips reset instants), 46 in all.

## 2026-10-08 — v0.3.5 (engine v0.2.2; presets v1.7)
- **Engine E-14:** a consistency rule can now be measured against the **profit target** rather than profit earned, with the firm's "the target rises, the account does not fail" semantics. Topstep's Trading Combine rule (best day at or below 55% of the target, from Topstep's own help centre) is therefore modelled: the three Topstep calculators show the raised target when a single day is too large. Agreed with the second engineering desk the same day; vectors pinned on both sides.
- **Engine E-15:** a day sitting *exactly* on a consistency cap, or exactly on a profitable-day threshold, now reads as meeting it. The old comparison could tip an exact 55% into "exceeded" by a floating-point hair (1,650 of 3,000 is 55.00000000000001 in binary). Found by reading the other desk's fix for the same class of defect.
- **FundingPips 1-Step Flex** added, two calculators (2% and 3% daily-loss configurations), from the firm's own page: 12% target, 12% static max on the lower of equity and balance, no minimum trading days.
- Topstep 50K/100K/150K re-issued as new dated presets carrying the consistency rule; the morning's versions are retained as superseded.
