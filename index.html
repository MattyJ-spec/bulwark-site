    Why it's different

    Pre-trade, not post-mortem

    Journals import yesterday's statement. Bulwark computes the ceiling for the trade you're about to take — the smaller of the risk you want and the room you have above the STOP line.

    The right anchor

    FTMO uses balance at midnight Prague; The5ers the higher of closing balance and equity; FundedNext the day-start balance. Get the anchor wrong and your floor is wrong by hundreds. Bulwark gets it right per firm.

    Receipts, not vibes

    Every rule preset carries a verified-as-of date and source links, and every number can show its working. The cockpit's receipts log (coming with the launch) will record what the rules said on the day, so a breach dispute rests on evidence rather than memory.

    What Bulwark will never do
    • Recommend a trade. No signals, no direction, no "buy here". Ever.
    • Touch your money or your logins. No broker credentials, no custody, no auto-trading in the free tools.
    • Promise outcomes. No pass rates, no profit claims, no testimonials about results.
    • Take affiliate money from firms or brokers. The rule data has to stay honest; that means no incentives to flatter anyone.
    • Show stale data as live. Every figure says what it's computed from and when.

    Before you trust a number here: every rule comes from the firm’s own pages, dated and linked; each calculator shows the date its rules were last read there, the changelog records every full re-read and what it found, and a rule changes only as a new dated version; the arithmetic runs through an engine verified against 198 independent vectors and audited by a second engine; where the tool cannot resolve something it refuses rather than guesses.
