FTMO Futures — Growth Account $150K (evaluations created from 5 Nov 2026) survival calculator

For Evaluations created on or after Thursday 5 November 2026, 4:30 a.m. Eastern Time (FTMO's Futures Trading Update of 5 November 2026 says 'created'; its objectives page says 'purchased'). FTMO defines the Growth Account's maximum drawdown as a dollar figure — $4,500 on the $150K account (3% of the starting capital), reduced from $5,000 for Evaluations created from that moment. It trails the highest end-of-day balance and locks at the starting capital; your equity may not touch it at any point. Evaluations created before the cut-over keep $5,000, 'including the Sim-Funded and Live Accounts they lead to' — they have their own calculator. FTMO does not say which figure applies to the new account it issues after a Reset, or on the next billing date after a breach: treat such an account as this one ($4,500) until Account MetriX shows $5,000. Profit target $9,000. Consistency Rule (FTMO lists it per plan — Growth 40%): your Best Day may not represent more than 40% of your total profit, 'the cumulative sum of closed profits and losses across all trading days in the Evaluation phase'. Exceeding it 'is not treated as a rule breach' — you keep trading until the Best Day is 40% or less of total profit. The calculator derives total profit from the account size, your day-start balance and today's closed trades. FTMO limits the Growth evaluation to 15 contracts held at one time (ten micro contracts count as one); the max-safe-size figure below does not apply that cap. This page models the Evaluation only: FTMO's Sim-Funded Growth account keeps the same maximum drawdown but adds a 'Soft' Daily Loss Limit ($3,000 on this size) that closes positions and suspends trading for the day, and has no profit target or consistency rule — not modelled here. No daily loss limit in the Growth evaluation.
