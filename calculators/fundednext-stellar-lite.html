⚠ Not for a Stellar Lite account bought with the Maximum Loss Limit 10% add-on. That add-on raises the maximum loss limit from 8% to 10%; this page uses 8%, so on such an account the max-loss floor shown here is not yours (it is higher — less room than you have). Go by the maximum loss limit your dashboard shows.

Minimum trading days: 2 per phase for Challenges purchased or reset on or after 12 October 2026; 5 per phase for Challenges purchased or last reset before that date (FundedNext help-centre article 8019803; the General Rules page lists 2). FundedNext's older Stellar Lite rules article (9094072, dated 3 July 2026) still says 5 without naming the date — the dated article governs. Floors and targets are the same for both groups. Server time: FundedNext states GMT+3 during daylight-saving time and GMT+2 for the rest of the year (help-centre article 8019811, 8 Apr 2026); Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown. In the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard. Max loss: FundedNext's Stellar Lite article reads 'Your account balance or equity cannot fall below 92% of your initial balance'; the calculator flags a breach when EITHER your closed balance or your equity touches the level — the tighter of the two readings. A daily-loss breach closes the account (General Rules: 'Breaching it closes the account'). FundedNext sells a No Minimum Trading Days add-on at purchase on Stellar Lite, which removes the minimum trading days of the Challenge phases. FundedNext's Stellar Lite product page has also been served to automated page readers with a line saying that the on-demand (90%) reward option keeps a minimum of 5 trading days in each phase — on 21 November 2026 and again on 22 November 2026, though not on every read; the page as displayed in a browser shows 2 days and no such line. If you took an on-demand reward option, check your minimum trading days on your dashboard. FundedNext's Quick Strike requirement — the share of profit from trades closed within 30 seconds of opening 'must be maintained below 30% at all times' — is not modelled here.
