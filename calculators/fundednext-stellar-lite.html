Minimum trading days: 2 per phase for Challenges purchased or reset on or after 12 October 2026; 5 per phase for Challenges purchased or last reset before that date (FundedNext help-centre article 8019803; the General Rules page lists 2). FundedNext's older Stellar Lite rules article (9094072, dated 3 July 2026) still says 5 without naming the date — the dated article governs. Floors and targets are the same for both groups. Server time: FundedNext states GMT+3 during daylight-saving time and GMT+2 for the rest of the year (help-centre article 8019811, 8 Apr 2026); Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown. In the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard. Max loss: FundedNext's Stellar Lite article reads 'Your account balance or equity cannot fall below 92% of your initial balance'; the calculator flags a breach when EITHER your closed balance or your equity touches the level — the tighter of the two readings. A daily-loss breach closes the account (General Rules: 'Breaching it closes the account').
