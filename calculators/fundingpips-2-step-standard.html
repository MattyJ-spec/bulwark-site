Your daily floor, max floor, buffered STOP line and the largest position a full stop can survive — computed from FundingPips's published rules: 5% daily (of the day-start value, anchored to the higher of balance and equity); 10% max (static); target 8% / 5% on closed balance.

Daily loss 5% of the higher of opening balance and opening equity; max loss 10% of the starting size, static, breached if equity OR balance touches the level (Bulwark measures the lower of the two). Profit targets 8% / 5%; minimum 3 trading days per phase. FundingPips also sells this model with a 3% daily-loss add-on chosen at purchase — if you bought it, use the '2-Step Standard (3% daily add-on)' calculator. On a Master account FundingPips also has rules on the loss from a single trade idea: on its on-demand 100% split, the Striking System — a warning when that loss reaches 1% of the account, the fourth warning closing the account; and for accounts under its Legacy Rules (bought before 28 September 2026), a Risk Per Trade Idea limit on Master accounts with the 10% target and a Striking System trigger of 1.2% on Master accounts above $25K with the 8% target. This page does not model them, and the size it shows does not apply them. Reset time: FundingPips states 00:00 Platform Time with a seasonal offset — UTC+3 in summer (21:00 UTC) and UTC+2 in winter (22:00 UTC), winter time starting the first Sunday of November. Bulwark counts down to 5:00 p.m. New York time, which is the same moment all year except the week between the first and second Sundays of March (FundingPips gives the first Sunday of March for summer time; New York changes on the second): in that week FundingPips' stated reset comes one hour BEFORE this countdown reaches zero — confirm the reset on your dashboard.
