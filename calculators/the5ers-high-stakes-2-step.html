Profit targets read from the High Stakes page itself: Step 1 10%, Step 2 5% — the June FAQ's 8% / 5% no longer appears there. Minimum 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, profit measured as min(midnight balance, midnight equity) − previous day's balance. A separate 2-Step Plan Rules page states 3% daily — the firm's assistant says that applies to the standard plan, not High Stakes. Verify on your dashboard.

Reset: MT5 server time, GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer (firm's assistant); Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown — in the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard.
