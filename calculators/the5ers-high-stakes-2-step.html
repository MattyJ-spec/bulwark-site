⚠ The5ers' Program Rules table gives $50K and $100K High Stakes accounts a 4% maximum daily loss and an 8% maximum loss; its $2.5K to $25K accounts have 5% and 10%. The firm's FAQ (last update 16 Nov 2026) gives the limits as ranges (maximum loss 8% to 10%, daily 3% to 5%) and tells you to check the Program Rules, yet its worked example still uses a 5% limit on a $100K account. This page uses the Program Rules table — the tighter figures. Before this correction it showed 5% and 10% for every account size. Verify on your dashboard.
⚠ Not for Summer Plan accounts. The5ers' Summer Plan — its FAQ has also called the $200K option the "High Stakes Summer Plan" — has a 3% daily loss limit (and a 6% maximum loss on its 1-Step): on such an account this page shows more room than you have. If your dashboard shows a 3% daily limit and a 10% maximum loss, use the Summer Plan 2-Step calculator linked below; there is no calculator here for the Summer Plan 1-Step.

For $50K and $100K High Stakes accounts: daily loss 4% of the higher of the previous day's closing balance and closing equity, recalculated at 00:00 server time; maximum loss 8% of the initial balance. Profit targets shown are the New option's — Step 1 10%, Step 2 5%. The Classic option's Step 1 target is 8%: on a Classic account the Step 1 target shown here is higher than yours. Each step also needs 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, profit measured as min(midnight balance, midnight equity) − previous day's balance. The5ers' pages do not say whether an account bought before its table showed these figures keeps other limits — read yours from your dashboard. Reset: MT5 server time, GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer (The5ers' FAQ on the maximum loss and maximum daily loss, last update 16 Nov 2026); Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown — in the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard.
