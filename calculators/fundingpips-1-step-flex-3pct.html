FundingPips also sells the 1-Step Flex with a 2% daily loss limit, chosen at purchase: on such an account this page shows more daily room than you have — use the 2% calculator linked below. Not for a 1 Step Labs account (FundingPips lists it with a 1% daily loss limit and a 3% maximum loss that trails at end of day) or a Zero account (3% daily; a 5% maximum loss that trails): this page would show either more room than it has, and there is no calculator here for them. Go by the limits your dashboard shows, not by the name of the product.

Daily loss 3% of the higher of opening balance and opening equity; max loss 12% of the starting size, static, breached if equity OR balance touches the level (Bulwark measures the lower of the two). Single evaluation phase with a 12% profit target and no minimum trading days. FundingPips sells the 1-Step Flex with a 2% or a 3% daily loss limit chosen at purchase — use the calculator that matches what you bought. On a Master account FundingPips also has rules on the loss from a single trade idea: on its on-demand 100% split, the Striking System — a warning when that loss reaches 1% of the account, the fourth warning closing the account; and for accounts under its Legacy Rules (bought before 28 September 2026), the Striking System on every Master account, not only on the 100% split. This page does not model them, and the size it shows does not apply them. Reset time: FundingPips states 00:00 Platform Time with a seasonal offset — UTC+3 in summer (21:00 UTC) and UTC+2 in winter (22:00 UTC), winter time starting the first Sunday of November. Bulwark counts down to 5:00 p.m. New York time, which is the same moment all year except the week between the first and second Sundays of March (FundingPips gives the first Sunday of March for summer time; New York changes on the second): in that week FundingPips' stated reset comes one hour BEFORE this countdown reaches zero — confirm the reset on your dashboard.
