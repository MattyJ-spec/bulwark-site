For accounts directly initiated from orders created before 12 October 2026 (FTMO's trading update: before Monday 12 October 2026, 9:00 platform time, GMT+3) and not merged since — an account merged on or after 12 October 2026 moves to the Consistency rule: use the '1-Step (orders from 12 Oct 2026)' calculator. FTMO's Best Day Rule (legacy) — your Best Day may not represent more than 50% of your Positive Days' Profit, 'the sum of closed profits and losses from all profitable trading days'. Exceeding the limit is not a rule breach; you continue trading until the best day is 50% or less of that figure. Enter the total profit from your positive days, including today if it is positive. Accounts from orders created on or after 12 October 2026 are measured on total profit instead — use the '1-Step (orders from 12 Oct 2026)' calculator. FTMO's pages list no minimum-trading-days objective for the 1-Step (the 4-day rule is stated for the 2-Step phases).
