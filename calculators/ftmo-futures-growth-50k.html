FTMO Futures — Growth Account $50K (evaluation) survival calculator

Your max-loss floor, a buffered STOP line and the largest position a full stop can survive — computed from FTMO's published rules: no daily loss limit; $2,000 (4%) max (trailing your highest end-of-day balance, locks at initial); target $3,000 (6%) on closed balance.

FTMO defines the Growth Account's maximum drawdown as a dollar figure — $2,000 on the $50K account (4% of the starting capital); FTMO's 5 November 2026 update reduced the $100K and $150K figures for new Evaluations and left the $50K unchanged. It trails the highest end-of-day balance and locks at the starting capital; your equity may not touch it at any point. Profit target $3,000. Consistency Rule (FTMO lists it per plan — Growth 40%): your Best Day may not represent more than 40% of your total profit, 'the cumulative sum of closed profits and losses across all trading days in the Evaluation phase'. Exceeding it 'is not treated as a rule breach' — you keep trading until the Best Day is 40% or less of total profit. The calculator derives total profit from the account size, your day-start balance and today's closed trades. FTMO limits the Growth evaluation to 5 contracts held at one time (ten micro contracts count as one); the max-safe-size figure below does not apply that cap. This page models the Evaluation only: FTMO's Sim-Funded Growth account keeps the same maximum drawdown but adds a 'Soft' Daily Loss Limit ($1,000 on this size) that closes positions and suspends trading for the day, and has no profit target or consistency rule — not modelled here. No daily loss limit in the Growth evaluation.
