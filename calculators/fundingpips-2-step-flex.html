Daily loss 4% of the higher of opening balance and opening equity; max loss 12% of the starting size, static, breached if equity OR balance touches the level (Bulwark measures the lower of the two). 80%-split path: profit targets 10% / 8% with 1 minimum trading day per phase. The 95%-split path instead requires 3 profitable days (≥0.5% each) per phase — not modelled here; read it from your dashboard. These are FundingPips' rules for accounts bought from 28 September 2026 or reset since then. An account bought before that date and not reset since follows its Legacy Rules page: the same loss limits, but an 85% split with profit targets 10% / 6% — the Phase 2 target shown here is higher than yours — and 1 minimum trading day per phase (0 if bought before 26 August 2026 and never reset). On a Master account FundingPips also has rules on the loss from a single trade idea: on its on-demand 100% split, the Striking System — a warning when that loss reaches 1% of the account, the fourth warning closing the account; and for accounts under its Legacy Rules, a Risk Per Trade Idea limit on Master accounts (3% of the account at $25K, 2% above $25K). This page does not model them, and the size it shows does not apply them. Reset time: FundingPips states 00:00 Platform Time with a seasonal offset — UTC+3 in summer (21:00 UTC) and UTC+2 in winter (22:00 UTC), winter time starting the first Sunday of November. Bulwark counts down to 5:00 p.m. New York time, which is the same moment all year except the week between the first and second Sundays of March (FundingPips gives the first Sunday of March for summer time; New York changes on the second): in that week FundingPips' stated reset comes one hour BEFORE this countdown reaches zero — confirm the reset on your dashboard. The Legacy Rules page still gives the reset as 00:00 Platform Time (UTC+3), with no seasonal note; if the countdown on your dashboard and this one differ, go by the dashboard.
