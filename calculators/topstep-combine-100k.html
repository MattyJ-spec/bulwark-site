Topstep defines its limits in dollars: Daily Loss Limit $2,000 (2% of the account size; an objective, not a violation — hitting it flattens positions and locks trading until 5 PM CT), Maximum Loss Limit $3,000 (trails your highest end-of-day balance, never moves down, locks at the starting balance), profit target $6,000. Consistency target (Topstep art. 8284208): your single best day must stay at or below 55% of the profit target; exceeding it is not a violation — the target rises until the best day is back under the cap. The calculator models this (engine v0.2.2, E-14) and shows the raised target when it applies.
