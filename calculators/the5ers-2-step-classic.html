⚠ For the 2-Step option of The5ers' Summer Plan only — 3% daily, 10% maximum, the same on its $50K, $100K and $200K options. Not for the Summer Plan 1-Step: its maximum loss is 6%, and this page would show it more room than it has. An account bought on The5ers' High Stakes page has other limits — 4% daily and 8% maximum at $50K and $100K, 5% and 10% up to $25K: use the calculators linked below. The5ers' FAQ has also used the name "High Stakes Summer Plan" for a Summer Plan option, so go by the limits your dashboard shows, not by the name. On a $50K account, check the maximum loss on your dashboard before relying on the max-loss floor here: The5ers' FAQ page for the $50K Summer Plan lists the 2-Step Classic as "8%/5% Max Loss", while its Summer Plan table gives 10%. If 8% were the limit, the max-loss floor of a $50K account would be $46,000, not the $45,000 this page shows.

Daily loss 3% of the higher of the previous day's closing balance and closing equity; maximum loss 10% of the initial balance; profit targets 8% / 5% (Classic, which The5ers also sells as '8/5'). The Summer Plan page's table gives 3% daily and 10% maximum for each of its six 2-Step options — $50K, $100K and $200K, 10/5 and 8/5 (read on 21 November 2026 with each option selected). The5ers' 2-Step Plan Rules FAQ (last update 2 Nov 2026) states the same two limits for the $100,000 account. On the Pro account the Summer Plan adds a 50% consistency requirement, which this page does not model. Reset: the plan's own pages give no reset time; the firm's assistant (21 Sep 2026) states MT5 server time, GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer. Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown — in the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard.
