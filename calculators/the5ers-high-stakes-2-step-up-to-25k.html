⚠ For $2.5K, $5K, $10K and $25K High Stakes accounts only. The5ers' Program Rules table gives $50K and $100K accounts tighter limits — 4% daily and 8% maximum: for one of those, use the $50K / $100K calculator linked below. This page gives no figures for another account size. Not checked for an account that has been scaled up under The5ers' Scaling Plan (it runs to $500,000): the High Stakes pages we read do not state a scaled-up account's loss limits. Go by your dashboard. Summer Plan accounts ($50K, $100K, $200K; 3% daily) are not covered here either.

For $2.5K, $5K, $10K and $25K High Stakes accounts: daily loss 5% of the higher of the previous day's closing balance and closing equity, recalculated at 00:00 server time; maximum loss 10% of the initial balance. Profit targets shown are the New option's — Step 1 10%, Step 2 5%. The Classic option's Step 1 target is 8%: on a Classic account the Step 1 target shown here is higher than yours. Each step also needs 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, profit measured as min(midnight balance, midnight equity) − previous day's balance. The5ers' pages do not say whether an account bought before its table showed these figures keeps other limits — read yours from your dashboard. Reset: MT5 server time, GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer (The5ers' FAQ on the maximum loss and maximum daily loss, last update 16 Nov 2026); Bulwark uses the Europe/Athens zone for the countdown — in the few weeks each year when European and US daylight-saving dates differ, confirm the reset moment on your dashboard.
