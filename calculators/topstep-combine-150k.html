⚠ The Daily Loss Limit is optional at Topstep. This page applies the $3,000 limit Topstep offers at checkout for this size; if your account has no Daily Loss Limit, the daily figures and the STOP line here do not apply to it; a personal limit set in Risk Settings may be another amount. Not for a Static Trading Combine either: its Maximum Loss Limit is $6,000 at this size and never moves, where this page trails your highest end-of-day balance. Check your account's parameters.

Topstep defines its limits in dollars: Daily Loss Limit $3,000 where the account has one (2% of the account size; optional, and not a rule violation — hitting it flattens positions and blocks new trades until 5 PM CT), Maximum Loss Limit $4,500 (trails your highest end-of-day balance, never moves down, locks at the starting balance), profit target $9,000. Consistency target (Topstep art. 8284208): your single best day must stay at or below 55% of the profit target; exceeding it is not a violation — the target rises until the best day is back under the cap. The calculator models this (engine v0.2.2, E-14) and shows the raised target when it applies. Minimum trading days: 2 (Topstep's Trading Combine Parameters table, 19 Nov 2026). The same table lists a Maximum Position Size of 15 mini or 150 micro contracts at this size, which Topstep can tighten temporarily; the size shown here does not apply it.
